# Order-Cancellation Policy

Any order still in Placed status may be cancelled by the customer free of charge. Once an order has moved to Shipped, self-service cancellation is no longer possible, and the customer's options are to refuse delivery at the door or to raise a return after the order is Delivered. Orders cancelled before dispatch are refunded in full within 3 working days. If the cancelled order had already been billed to a credit card, the amount is reversed on the card rather than issued as a fresh refund, so the credit may take one full billing cycle to appear.
