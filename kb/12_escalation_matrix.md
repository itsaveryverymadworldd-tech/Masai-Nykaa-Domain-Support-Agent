# Customer-Support Escalation Matrix

The escalation matrix defines when an automated support agent must hand a conversation to a human, and to which tier. Tier 1 is the automated agent itself, and it handles policy questions and order-status lookups directly without escalation. A conversation escalates to Tier 2 when the order's escalation score is at or above the configured threshold, when a delayed order has a value above Rs.10,000, or when the customer has contacted support three or more times about the same order. Tier 3 is the resolution desk and handles legal threats, regulator or consumer-forum complaints, and repeat payment failures that survived a Tier 2 handling. Any request that mentions a data-privacy concern bypasses Tier 2 entirely and goes straight to Tier 3 because of the regulatory exposure.
