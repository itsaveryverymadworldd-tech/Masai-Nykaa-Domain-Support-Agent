FORMULA
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  escalation_score = status_urgency * (w_delay*delayed + w_recency*recency + w_value*value)

    w_delay    = 0.4   delayed flag (1.0 if shipment delayed else 0.0)
    w_recency  = 0.3   days_since_created normalised over 30 days
    w_value    = 0.3   order_value_inr saturating at Rs.25,000

  status_urgency multipliers:
      Delivered    0.55
      Placed       1.0
      Refunded     0.35
      Returned     0.85
      Shipped      1.0

  This is a weighted blend of three continuous components, not a
  boolean OR: two orders can both be delayed yet score differently
  depending on how stale and how valuable they are.

THRESHOLD JUSTIFICATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Chosen threshold = p80 of escalation_score = 0.3577
  Orders at or above it: 10/48 = 20.8% of the dataset
  Their days_since_created: median 13, IQR 8-22
  i.e. the threshold corresponds to the stalest ~20% of orders, which
  is precisely the population a support team wants a human to see.
